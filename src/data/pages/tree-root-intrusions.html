How hydro jetting may help

Specialized jetting nozzles can break up and flush root growth in a pipe that is safe to clean. A camera check is important because roots often point to a joint opening or a broken section; water alone will not seal that entry point. When to ask for a closer look

Slow drainage across several fixtures or a recurring main-line backup may suggest a root problem, but those symptoms do not identify its cause by themselves. Have the line checked before treating the blockage. Avoid assuming every root-filled line is a candidate for high-pressure cleaning.

Will hydro jetting stop tree roots permanently?
